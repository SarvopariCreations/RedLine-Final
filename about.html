About Red Lion Advisory
  • Red Lion Advisory is a strategic partner to private companies, family offices, and capital allocators. We bring decades of experience across capital markets, operations, and executive leadership to deliver practical, high-impact advisory services.
  • We advise clients through capital raises, investment diligence, operational challenges, executive level leadership and strategic pivots—helping them navigate complexity with clarity and confidence.

  • We integrate seamlessly into client organizations, delivering financial clarity, operational discipline, and consistent execution through our F.A.S.T. ™ approach (Finance, Analytics, Strategy, and Transformation).

  • Our team leads with discipline, insight, and urgency—translating strategy into action and ensuring no momentum is lost in moments that count.

  • We deliver OIPAAS (Operating & Investment Partner-as-a-Service) solutions— providing companies and allocators with an integrated platform for strategic planning, capital allocation, and execution support.
