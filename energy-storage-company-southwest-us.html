Challenge

The company needed bridge and seed-stage capital to appropriately scale manufacturing and execute commercialization

Lacked a cohesive strategic financial model and robust investor materials to attract institutional investors

RLA F.A.S.T.™ Approach

Financial Modeling & Structuring: Built an institutional-quality financial model detailing growth projections, unit economics, and capital usage, showcasing transformative investment opportunities

Accelerated Investor Preparation: Prepared a compelling investor deck highlighting the company’s emerging leadership in the energy storage sector

Streamlined Due Diligence: Led a detailed process that included executing a comprehensive one- and two-year roadmap covering corporate, commercial, and technological milestones

Outcome

Delivered an investor-ready financial model, solidifying credibility and expediting deal discussions

Facilitated a swift and efficient due diligence process, establishing strong alignment with institutional partners

Optimized capital structuring, preserving long-term flexibility and minimizing dilution
