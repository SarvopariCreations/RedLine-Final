Typical Capital Raise Process

We guide clients through the full capital raise lifecycle—from strategic preparation to targeted investor outreach and final negotiations. Our hands-on approach ensures materials are market-ready, investor conversations are managed with precision, and deal execution is tightly coordinated to maximize outcomes..

Phase 1 Preparation & Strategy
  • Define capital needs and investor profile
  • Align on scope, structure, and timeline
  • Conduct diligence and financial review
  • Incorporate institutional quality, robust financial forecasting and modeling
  • Develop pitch materials
  • Populate and manage data room
  • Set initial terms and raise strategy
Phase 2 Market Outreach & Engagement
  • Build and prioritize investor list
  • Manage outreach and responses
  • Coordinate intro calls and pitches
  • Support Q&A and feedback loops
  • Drive term sheet discussions
  • Refine strategy from investor feedback
Phase 3 Negotiation & Close
  • Align on financial structure and terms
  • Manage investor diligence process
  • Lead legal and documentation
  • Lead capital stack execution
  • Oversee timely closing, ensuring execution
  • Prepare for post-close integration
