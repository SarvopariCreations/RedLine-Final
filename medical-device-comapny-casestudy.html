How we can help you?

Challenge

The company experienced high growth and strong market demand but lacked a robust financial infrastructure and transformative strategic leadership

Reliance on fewer than five core clients created significant revenue concentration risk

Accounting processes were disorganized and unreliable, hindering both financial visibility and investor credibility

Additional capital was needed to scale operations, diversify the client base, and establish a foothold as a long-term market leader

RLA F.A.S.T.™ Approach

Financial Realignment & Controls: Assumed the CFO role, instituting rigorous financial controls and structured reporting to bolster transparency and investor confidence

Accelerated Capital Raise: Led negotiations for an investment that balanced operational flexibility with sustainable growth

Streamlined Accounting & Reporting: Executed a transformative overhaul of the accounting system

transitioning to GAAP-compliant accrual accounting, automating reporting, and optimizing cost tracking

Targeted Client Diversification: Expanded the client base from under five to more than fifteen, significantly reducing revenue risk and stabilizing cash flow

Outcome

Secured the necessary capital, negotiating, structuring, and executing a competitively priced term debt over a 10-year period to enable rapid growth in operations and customer acquisition

Reduced customer concentration risk, creating more predictable revenues by more than doubling the customers over the last trailing twelve months (TTM)

Built a transparent, scalable financial reporting system, supporting continued growth and investor relations.

Optimized the company’s capital structure, ensuring an efficient balance between debt and equity to support sustainable expansion.

Introduced merger and roll-up opportunities, enhancing the company’s transformative growth strategy.
