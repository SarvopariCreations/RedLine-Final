Family Office Advisory

Red Lion Advisory serves as a high-impact partner to family offices, delivering institutional-grade diligence, strategic insight, and hands-on leadership across the private investment landscape.

Family offices are constantly navigating a flood of inbound opportunities—often with unclear risk, uneven quality, and limited transparency. RLA acts as a trusted, independent partner, rigorously evaluating deals, challenging assumptions, and uncovering what others miss. Beyond diligence, we offer retained advisory to help manage portfolio complexity, rebalance capital, and guide companies through key transitions. Whether you need investment insight or embedded leadership, we operate as a strategic extension of your team.

Retained Strategic & Portfolio Advisory

Ongoing access to our team for deal screening, portfolio monitoring, capital allocation decisions, and fractional C-suite leadership.

Independent Diligence & Valuation

Institutional-grade financial modeling, market analysis, operational reviews, and scenario testing—focused on clarity, risk, and value creation.

Private Company Investment Access

Exclusive and referred deal flow across growth-stage, late-stage, and sponsor-backed companies seeking capital, operational advisory, or liquidity.

Diverse Offerings

We advise clients across every stage of the investment lifecycle—from sourcing and evaluating opportunities to optimizing capital structures, improving operations, and navigating liquidity events. Our multidisciplinary team delivers the insight, rigor, and leadership required to drive value creation and mitigate risk in dynamic markets.
